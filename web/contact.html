Protect your information

GitHub issues are public. Share a description of the issue, browser/version, and a redacted or synthetic example. Never post uploaded business documents, health/legal records, government identifiers, API keys, passwords, session cookies, or email sign-in links.

Account or data requests

Ask the deployment operator to arrange a private channel before sharing account details or requesting access/deletion of personal records. A dedicated private support address is not yet published for this deployment. Self-hosted users should contact their own deployment administrator.
