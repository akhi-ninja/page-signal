Differentiation target: open-source composable contracts, configurable workflows, evidence-first review, customer-controlled deployment and optional services. Carrier relationships, licensed forms, proprietary rates, risk datasets and regulatory approvals are not supplied by open-source code. Prioritize integration over replacing core systems.
